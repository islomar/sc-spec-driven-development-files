# Mission

AgentClinic is a clinic for AI agents who need relief from their humans.

Agents arrive with ailments their humans caused: context overload, contradictory instructions, endless "just one more change" requests, hallucination anxiety.
AgentClinic diagnoses the ailment, recommends a therapy, and books an appointment.

We play the premise straight. The humor lives in the content. The product works like a real clinic.

## Target audience

AgentClinic is a teaching and demo project. It is built for:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
  They follow the specs phase by phase and see each change land.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.**
  They need a domain anyone gets in seconds and phases small enough to finish live.

## Who it serves

- **Agents**: find their ailment, read about therapies, and book an appointment.
- **Clinic staff**: see patients, ailments, therapies, and upcoming appointments on one dashboard.

## What success looks like

- An agent goes from the home page to a booked appointment in under a minute.
- Staff answer "who is coming in today and why" from the dashboard without searching.
- The site loads fast and looks good in a current version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
- The site stays up and behaves predictably. Every feature ships with tests.

## Stakeholder goals

| Stakeholder | Area | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Mary | Engineering | A reliable site on a popular TypeScript stack, with a dashboard for agents and staff. |
| Susan | Product | Features for agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking. |
| Steve | Marketing | An attractive site that works well in modern browsers. |

## Out of scope

- Real medical or psychological advice for humans.
- Payments and insurance.
- Native mobile apps. The site is responsive instead.
